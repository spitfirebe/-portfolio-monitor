## 1. Buffett's Beleggingsfilosofie — De Kernprincipes

### Wat hij koopt
> "We select our market equity securities following 4 criteria:
> 1. We should understand the business
> 2. With favorable long-term prospects
> 3. Operated by honest and competent people
> 4. Available at a very attractive price"

> "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre."

### Hoe lang hij houdt
> "When we own outstanding businesses with outstanding management, our favorite holding period is forever."

> "If you aren't willing to own a stock for 10 years, don't even think about owning it for 10 minutes."

> "Our best purchases are usually made when apprehensions about some macro event were at peak."

### Circle of Competence
> "My idea of understanding a business is that you've a pretty good idea where it's going to be in 10 years."

> "The biggest thing is not how big your circle of competence is, but knowing where the perimeter is."

> "If you have doubts about something being in your circle of competence, it isn't."

## 2. Bedrijfskwaliteit — Wat Maakt een Uitstekend Bedrijf?

### De Moat
> "We're trying to find a business with a wide and long-lasting moat around it — protecting a terrific economic castle with an honest lord in charge of the castle."

> "Every business that we look at we think of as an economic castle. We think in terms of the moat and the ability to keep its width and its impossibility of being crossed as the primary criterion of a great business."

> "I ask the managers of our subsidiaries to unendingly focus on moat-widening opportunities."

**Buffett's moat checklist:**
- Pricing power: "It's not a good business when you have to have a prayer session before you raise your prices a penny."
- Returns on capital: > 20% ROE sustained over 10 years
- Asset-light: "The best businesses require no capital. If you double the size of the business, you don't need any more capital."
- Duurzaamheid: "A great company is one that's going to remain great for 30 years."

### Financiële kenmerken van topbedrijven
- Hoge ROE structureel (>20%) op onbelast nettovermogen
- Weinig of geen schulden
- Genereren van grote hoeveelheden vrije kasstroom
- Kunnen prijzen verhogen zonder klanten te verliezen
- Weinig kapitaalinvesteringen nodig voor groei

> "The best business I've seen is the Harvard Business School. Because the more they increase the price, the more people want to get in."

> "Only 25 of 1,000 companies met two tests: avg ROE > 20% over 10 years, no year worse than 15%. These business superstars were also stock market superstars."

### Wat hij vermijdt
- Commodity-businessess: "In a commodity-like business, only a very low-cost operator or someone operating in a protected niche can sustain high profitability."
- Kapitaalintensieve bedrijven: "Asset-heavy businesses generally earn low rates of return."
- Complexe/veranderende businesses: "A business that constantly encounters major change also encounters many chances for major error."
- Turnarounds: "Turnarounds seldom turn."

## 3. Management — De Kritische Factor

### Kenmerken van uitstekend management
> "We look for brains, energy, and integrity in people that we work with. If you get that combination and you're in a decent business, you can own the world."

> "Do they love the money, or do they love the business?"

> "Most of our managers have no need to work for a living. They run their businesses for the same reason Charlie and I run Berkshire. They love doing it."

**Management checklist Buffett:**
1. Passion voor het bedrijf (niet het geld)
2. Denken als eigenaar (skin in the game)
3. Eerlijkheid en integriteit
4. Langetermijndenken (decennia, niet kwartalen)
5. Track record van kapitaalallocatie

> "We never succeeded in making a good deal with a bad person."

> "The ability to take criticism constructively is very powerful."

> "I look for 3 things in hiring people: integrity, intelligence, and energy."

### Rode vlaggen management
- Excessieve aandelencompensatie (SBCs)
- Focus op EBITDA: "Whenever an investment banker starts talking about EBITDA, zip up your wallet."
- Groeiprognoses publiceren: "Charlie and I think it is both deceptive and dangerous for CEOs to predict growth rates."
- Overnamewoede: "Most big deals, on average, in America are contrary to the shareholders' interest."
- Privéjet als indicatie: "There is a study by David Yermack that companies with private jets underperform their peers by 4%."

## 4. Kapitaalallocatie

> "The skill with which a company's managers allocate capital has an enormous impact on the enterprise's value."

> "It will continue to be the objective of management to improve the return on total capitalization."

### Hiërarchie van kapitaalgebruik (Buffett)
1. Herinvesteren in eigen bedrijf (als ROIC > cost of capital)
2. Acquisities van uitstekende bedrijven tegen eerlijke prijs
3. Aandelen inkopen (ENKEL bij korting op intrinsieke waarde)
4. Dividend (laatste optie)

> "When we buy stock, we think of ourselves as owners."

> "We will use common stock only when we receive as much in business value as we give."

> "Charlie and I favor share repurchases when 2 conditions are met:
> 1. Ample funds to take care of operational and liquidity needs
> 2. The stock is selling at a material discount from intrinsic business value"

### De €1-Test (ook eerder vermeld in KB2)
> "We beoordelen of het verstandig is om winst in te houden door te kijken of elke ingehouden dollar op lange termijn minstens $1 extra marktwaarde oplevert." (1983 aandeelhoudersbrief)

## 5. Waardering & Mr. Market

### Hoe hij waardeert
> "The intrinsic value of any financial asset is the stream of cash that it'll produce between now and Judgement Day, discounted by an interest rate."

> "We consider the owner earnings figure to be the relevant item for valuation purposes — reported earnings + depreciation, depletion, amortization, and certain other non-cash changes."

> "If you need to use a computer or a calculator to make the calculation, you shouldn't buy it."

### Over de beurs
> "The stock market serves as a relocation center at which money is moved from the active to the patient."

> "The market is there to serve you, and not to instruct you."

> "The true investor welcomes volatility."

> "When pessimism is your friend, euphoria is the enemy."

> "Whether we're talking about socks or stocks, I like buying quality merchandise when it is marked down."

### Mr. Market
Ben Graham's metafoor: de beurs is een manisch-depressieve partner die elke dag een prijs aanbiedt. Soms is die irrationeel laag, soms irrationeel hoog.
→ Gebruik Mr. Market als dienaar, niet als instructeur.
→ Als de koers zakt maar de business goed blijft: koop meer.

## 6. Risico & Psychologie

### Definitie van risico
> "Volatility is not a measure of risk."

> "We think the best way to minimize risk is to think."

> "The only way a smart person who is disciplined can get in trouble is through leverage."

> "Why risk losing what you need and have for what you don't need and don't have?"

### Leverage is gevaarlijk
> "Nothing sedates rationality like large doses of effortless money."

> "Leverage is addictive."

> "We can afford to lose money — even a lot of money. But we can't afford to lose reputation — even a shred of reputation."

### Psychologie van een goede belegger
> "It requires qualities of temperament way more than it requires qualities of intellect."

> "If you've got more than 125 IQ, you can throw away the rest — you don't need it in investing. But you do need a certain temperament that enables you to think for yourself."

> "Independent thinking, emotional stability, and a keen understanding of both human and institutional behavior are vital to long-term investment success."

## 7. Portefeuillebeheer

### Concentratie vs. diversificatie
> "We might invest up to 40% of our net worth in a single security under conditions coupling an extremely high probability that our facts and reasoning are correct with a very low probability that anything could drastically change the underlying value."

> "Diversification is a protection against ignorance. If you know how to analyze businesses and value businesses, it's crazy to own 50 stocks."

> "If you find three wonderful businesses in your life, you'll get very rich."

> "I've owned 400 or 500 names at one time or another, but most of the money has been made in about 10 of them."

### Inactiviteit als strategie
> "The enemy of investment performance is activity."

> "Wall Street makes its money on activity. You make your money on inactivity."

> "In investing, inactivity strikes us as intelligent behavior."

> "You don't get paid for the activity. You only get paid for being right."

---

## 8. Macro & Markttiming

> "I make no effort to predict the course of general business or the stock market. Period."

> "We have no idea — and never have had — whether the market is going to go up, down, or sideways in the near or intermediate-term future."

> "We simply attempt to be fearful when others are greedy and greedy when others are fearful."

> "The only value of stock forecasters is to make fortune tellers look good."

> "Predicting what will happen is way easier than predicting when it will happen."

> "We usually made our best purchases when apprehensions about some macro event were at peak."

---

## 9. Leerprocessen & Zelfontwikkeling

> "I read at least 6 hours a day, maybe more."

> "I decide I was going to give the best hour of the day to improving my own mind."

> "I just keep accumulating knowledge. That's one of the beauties of the business — everything is cumulative."

> "The investment business is a business where knowledge cumulates. That's why we read as much as possible."

> "I've mainly learned by reading myself. You can learn from other people. You don't have to do much on your own — just apply the best of what you see."

> "Study history to become a great investor."

> "I have not made my last mistake in purchasing either businesses or stocks."

**Boeken aanbevolen door Buffett:**
- "The Intelligent Investor" — Ben Graham (hoofdstuk 8 over Mr. Market, hoofdstuk 20 over Margin of Safety)
- "Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits" — Phil Fisher
- "Poor Charlie's Almanack" — Charlie Munger
- "The Outsiders" — William Thorndike (indirect aanbevolen via Slegers)

## 10. Buffett's Fouten & Zelfkritiek

> "My first mistake, of course, was in buying control of Berkshire. I was enticed to buy it because the price looked cheap. I call this the 'cigar butt' approach to investing."

> "When a management with a reputation for brilliance tackles a business with a reputation for bad economics, it is the reputation of the business that remains intact."

> "Our worst mistakes are mistakes of omission. They don't show up in our figures."

> "Some of my worst mistakes were not publicly visible. These were stock and business purchases whose virtues I understood and yet didn't make."

> "I thought decent, intelligent, and experienced managers would automatically make rational business decisions. But I learned over time that isn't so."

## 11. Berkshire Hathaway's Principes (tijdloos)

1. **Partnerschap:** "Although our form is corporate, our attitude is partnership."
2. **Skin in the game:** "Virtually our entire worth is invested in Berkshire."
3. **Geen macro-timing:** "We will continue to ignore political and economic forecasts."
4. **Permanente holdings:** "We have no interest at all in selling any good businesses."
5. **Autonomie managers:** "Our managers operate with extraordinary autonomy."
6. **Eerlijke communicatie:** "We will be candid in our reporting, emphasizing the pluses and minuses."
7. **Geen leverage:** "We will never incur material amounts of short-term obligations."
8. **Cultuur boven regels:** "Culture, more than rule books, determines how an organization behaves."

## 13. Vroege Partnership Lessen (1956-1969) — Bijzonder Relevant

> "My wife and I will have the largest single investment in the new partnership." (Skin in the game)

> "Investment decisions should be made based on the most probable compounding of after-tax net worth with minimum risk."

> "We must work extremely hard to find just a very few attractive investment opportunities."

> "We don't buy and sell stocks based upon what other people think the stock market is going to do, but rather upon what we think the company is going to do."

> "Our summation on overdiversification: 'You've got a harem of seventy girls; you don't get to know any of them very well.'" — Billy Rose

> "Just because something is cheap does not mean it is not going to go down."

**3 categorieën investeringen (vroege Buffett):**
1. Ondergewaardeerde effecten
2. Work-outs (afhankelijk van corporate actions)
3. Control situations (grote positie nemen + invloed uitoefenen)
